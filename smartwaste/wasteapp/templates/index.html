Our Services

Complete Waste Management Solutions

Designed to connect every important part of community waste management through one digital system.

Property Management

Register properties and maintain organized property information for efficient waste collection and property-wise history.

Waste Collection

Sanitation workers can verify properties, record collection activity and categorize collected waste.

Illegal Dumping Reports

Citizens can report inappropriate dumping locations and provide photo evidence for administrative review.

Bulk Waste Pickup

Request collection for furniture, appliances, garden waste and other large discarded materials.

Food Redistribution

Connect surplus food donors with organizations that can request and redistribute usable food.

Administrative Monitoring

Administrators can monitor properties, workers, collections, reports and community activities.
